# HumanPen notes — AI writing report literacy

Short, citable notes on **how to read an AI writing / similarity report** and on
**paragraph-level document revision**. Written for international students, tutors and
anyone who receives a detector report and has to decide what it actually says.

The published site is at **https://lwjuolpenncjbs.github.io/humanpen-blog/** —
[all 858 notes, grouped by language](https://lwjuolpenncjbs.github.io/humanpen-blog/all-notes.html).

## What this material is

- A **reading procedure** for reports in the Turnitin / iThenticate family: what the
  headline percentage has as its denominator, what the *asterisk* confidence flag means,
  why coverage matters more than the number, and why two reports from different detector
  versions are not comparable.
- A **document-revision procedure** for the paragraphs a report actually flags: keep the
  layout, keep the citations, keep the file editable, and re-import a fresh report after
  each pass instead of guessing.
- Notes on the **office-hours conversation**: which submission attempt was scored, what to
  bring, how to ask for the full export rather than arguing from a screenshot.

## What this material is not

- It does **not** promise a score, a percentage, or a pass. No page here says "this will get
  you under X%". Detector output is a classifier estimate, and any claim of a guaranteed
  result would be false.
- It is **not** a guide to evading detection. The revision notes describe editing your own
  draft so that the writing is actually yours — they assume the argument and the sources are
  yours to begin with.
