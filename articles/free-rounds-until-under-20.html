Free rewrite rounds until the report shows * under 20%

Charles Li / HumanPen

On the HumanPen product page, the commitment is concrete: you pay for words actually rewritten; if a re-check still shows 20% or higher, import the new Turnitin or iThenticate report and remaining flagged passages are rewritten for free until the report shows * under 20%. That is a billing and workflow promise about free rounds on still-flagged spans—not a promise that any detector will “pass” you forever.

This is distinct from import-report-rewrite-flagged-only (how to target flagged spans), from freeze-citations-numbers-formulas (what to leave untouched), and from every retired meeting-literacy theme. Here the literacy move is the paid pass → re-import → free rounds on leftover flags → * under 20% loop as HumanPen describes it.

This note explains the product framing honestly. It does not guarantee a clean next card, a school policy outcome, or that the asterisk band means “cleared of AI use.”

Better order

  1. Import the first AI writing report; rewrite only flagged continuous prose (paid on rewritten words).
  2. Re-submit or re-check on your school’s Turnitin / iThenticate surface; download the new full export.
  3. If the new report still shows ≥20%, re-import that report into HumanPen.
  4. Run free rewrite rounds on the remaining flagged passages—not a whole-file wash—until the imported report shows * under 20%.
  5. Freeze citations, numbers, formulas, and layout on every round. Read coverage and legend before you celebrate the asterisk.

What the homepage example is saying

HumanPen illustrates a path like: Report 1 at a high AI % → paid rewrite → Report 2 still ≥20% → free round (0 credits) → Report 3 showing * under 20%. Treat that as a product workflow diagram, not as your personal score forecast. Detectors update; short papers and template prose behave differently; your next export can disagree.

What this is not

  • Not a guarantee the detector will pass, clear you, or that * under 20% is a misconduct verdict.
  • Not unlimited free rewriting of unflagged or whole-document text.
  • Not “undetectable,” bypass, or a substitute for your course integrity process.

Where HumanPen fits

HumanPen at humanpen.net pairs document-level flagged-span rewrites with that free-rounds commitment after a paid pass when the re-imported report stays at or above 20%. Complementary to report-reading guides and to importing the report before you rewrite. No promised next percentage beyond the product’s own free-round rule.

Remember

  • Paid rewrite → re-import if still ≥20% → free rounds on leftover flags.
  • Target remaining flagged passages, not the whole file again.
  • * under 20% is the product stop condition—not a guarantee of school clearance.
  • Honest tools do not sell “undetectable.”
