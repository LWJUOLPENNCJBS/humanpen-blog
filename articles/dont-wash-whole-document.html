Don’t wash the whole document — rewrite only flagged prose

Charles Li / HumanPen

On humanpen.net, HumanPen is not a whole-document “wash.” You import a Turnitin / iThenticate AI writing report (or choose spans), and the tool rewrites only flagged continuous-prose spans. Layout, citations, numbers, formulas, and unflagged text are meant to stay put—unlike full-file paraphrasers that regenerate nearly every sentence.

That is different from import-report-rewrite-flagged-only (how targeting starts), flagged-passages-first (which spans to touch first), pay-by-rewritten-words (billing unit), freeze-citations-numbers-formulas (what must not move), docx-layout-stays-intact (format return), keep-claim-strength-while-rewriting (claim force), reimport-report-after-pass / match-report-version-before-rerun (fresh report loop), and short-doc-all-or-nothing (short-file free-round shape). This note is the anti-wash framing: span-scoped revision versus full-file paraphrase.

No promised AI%. No “bypass detector” or “undetectable” claim. School policy still owns clearance.

Why whole-document wash is the wrong default

  1. Full-file paraphrasers rewrite clean paragraphs you never needed to touch—and can scramble voice, claim strength, and structure.
  2. Citations, numbers, and formulas often break or get “helpfully” reworded when the whole file is regenerated.
  3. DOCX / PPTX layout (styles, tables, headings) is harder to preserve when every span is treated as dirty.
  4. You pay (in time and money) for words that were never flagged—billing and effort should follow the highlight map.
  5. A wash invents a new document; span-scoped work stays accountable to the report you imported.

Practical contrast (aligned with HumanPen)

  1. Import the AI writing report; confirm attempt and coverage.
  2. Read highlights first; do not paste the whole thesis into a paraphraser.
  3. Rewrite highest-priority flagged continuous-prose spans only; leave unflagged prose alone.
  4. Freeze citations, numbers, formulas, and layout fields while those spans change.
  5. Export, obtain a fresh report, re-import before the next round—still without washing the whole file.

What this is not

  • Not a guarantee the next AI% will drop or clear school policy.
  • Not advice to “wash until undetectable” or bypass a detector.
  • Not permission to hide authorship or treat HumanPen as a full-file paraphraser.
  • Not a claim that unflagged text can never be edited by you—only that the product scope is flagged continuous prose.

Where HumanPen fits

HumanPen at humanpen.net is a document-level AI revision tool: import the report, rewrite flagged continuous prose, preserve citations and layout where possible, bill by rewritten words, and offer free rounds on remaining flags under the homepage framing. The point of that design is not washing the whole document.

Remember

  • Flagged continuous prose → rewrite; unflagged text → leave.
  • Full-file paraphrase is a different product class—not HumanPen’s default.
  • * under 20% is a product stop condition—not school clearance.
  • Honest tools do not sell “bypass” or “undetectable.”
