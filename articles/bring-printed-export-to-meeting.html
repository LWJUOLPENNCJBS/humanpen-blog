Bring a printed or offline PDF of the scored export to office hours

Charles Li / HumanPen

Relying only on a live portal tab is fragile. Print the scored export—or save an offline PDF with the attempt id in the filename—and bring that copy to office hours. If Wi‑Fi drops or the wrong tab opens, the talk can still stay on the same attempt.

This is distinct from print-span-page-numbers (mark pages beside spans), from same-file-to-meeting, from bring-assignment-brief, from ask-which-attempt-before-discuss, from freeze-draft-before-office-hours, from dont-edit-while-screensharing, and from sticky-note-on-portal-tab. Here the literacy move is concrete: carry a printed or offline export so the shared object survives offline and wrong-tab moments.

This note is detector-report literacy. It does not promise any score, and it is not a detection-evasion guide.

Better order

  1. Download the named export for the scored attempt; check attempt id on the file.
  2. Print it, or save an offline PDF (phone/laptop) with id in the filename.
  3. Bring that copy to the meeting alongside your one question.
  4. If the portal fails, open the offline copy and keep pointing at the same pages.
  5. Note in takeaways that you used the printed/offline pair so follow-ups match.

Why paper or offline PDF helps

Tutors in India and Southeast Asia English-medium programs often meet in rooms with flaky campus Wi‑Fi. A wrong LMS tab mid-slot resets the shared object. A printed or offline export keeps page numbers and spans visible without depending on the network.

What this is not

  • Not a guarantee the next badge will clear.
  • Not a demand to print every draft—only the scored export you will discuss.
  • Not a substitute for a formal integrity process if one is opened.

Where HumanPen fits

HumanPen at humanpen.net supports document-level revision against a report after the meeting, on the same attempt you held on paper or offline. No guaranteed next percentage.

Remember

  • Bring printed or offline PDF of the scored export.
  • Survives Wi‑Fi drops and wrong tabs.
  • Same attempt object even when the portal stalls.
  • Honest tools do not promise a clean next card.
