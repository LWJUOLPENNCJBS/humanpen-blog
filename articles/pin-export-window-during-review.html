Pin the scored export window on top while you take review notes

Charles Li / HumanPen

You open notes and the export disappears under Word. Mid-sentence you re-find the wrong PDF. During review, pin the scored export window (always-on-top / sticky / float) so it stays visible while you write—don’t bury it under the draft editor.

This is distinct from sticky-note-on-portal-tab (note on the LMS tab), from full-screen-export-during-review, from one-pdf-window-during-review, from close-extra-tabs-before-review, from dont-edit-while-screensharing, from mute-chat-during-flag-review, and from write-attempt-id-on-notes. Here the literacy move is concrete: keep the scored export pinned on top while notes sit beside it.

This note is detector-report literacy. It does not promise any score, and it is not a detection-evasion guide.

Better order

  1. Open the named scored export; confirm attempt id.
  2. Enable always-on-top / pin / float for that window (OS or PDF viewer).
  3. Open your notes beside—not over—the pinned export.
  4. Copy span page numbers into notes while the export stays visible.
  5. Unpin only after takeaways; then reopen the editor if needed.

Why pinning beats digging

Tutors in India and Southeast Asia English-medium programs point at what they can see. When the export is buried, students re-open a draft and the shared object silently swaps. A pinned window keeps evidence and notes in one glance.

What this is not

  • Not a guarantee the next badge will clear.
  • Not permission to edit the pinned export mid-review—view and note only.
  • Not a substitute for a formal integrity process if one is opened.

Where HumanPen fits

HumanPen at humanpen.net supports document-level revision against a report after a review where the named export stayed visible beside your notes. No guaranteed next percentage.

Remember

  • Pin / always-on-top the scored export during review.
  • Don’t bury it under the draft editor.
  • Notes beside the pinned window—not over it.
  • Honest tools do not promise a clean next card.
