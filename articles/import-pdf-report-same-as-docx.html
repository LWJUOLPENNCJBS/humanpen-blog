Import a PDF report the same way as a DOCX report

Charles Li / HumanPen

On humanpen.net, importing a Turnitin / iThenticate-style similarity or AI writing report works the same whether the export is PDF or DOCX. HumanPen maps flagged spans onto your draft and rewrites only those passages—without washing the whole file.

That complements import-report-rewrite-flagged-only (report drives targeting), match-report-span-to-rewrite (rewrite the highlighted span), and docx-layout-stays-intact (layout fidelity on your draft). It differs from portal-vs-pdf coverage literacy, recheck-after-detector-update (fresh report after vendor change), only-flagged-continuous-prose / leave-unflagged-prose-alone (scope), and pay-by-rewritten-words (billing). This note names format-agnostic report import: PDF or DOCX report → same flagged-span workflow.

No promised next AI%. No “bypass detector” or “undetectable” claim. School policy still owns clearance.

Why PDF and DOCX reports should behave the same

  1. Schools export reports as PDF or DOCX depending on portal UI—students should not learn two products.
  2. The unit of work is the flagged continuous-prose span, not the container format of the report file.
  3. Whole-file wash tools ignore that distinction and treat any upload as text to scrub.
  4. Honest tools map highlights onto your working draft (usually DOCX) regardless of report packaging.
  5. Confirm attempt identity and coverage still matter before the first rewrite—format does not excuse a mismatched attempt.

Practical workflow (aligned with HumanPen)

  1. Export the AI / similarity report from Turnitin or iThenticate as PDF or DOCX—whichever your portal offers.
  2. Import that report into HumanPen; confirm attempt identity and coverage match the draft you will edit.
  3. Let HumanPen map flagged continuous-prose spans onto your draft; rewrite only those spans.
  4. Keep citations, numbers, formulas, tables, and layout intact; do not wash unflagged neighbors.
  5. Re-export your draft; re-import a fresh report for the next round. Free rounds (if any) still apply only to still-flagged prose under the homepage framing.

What this is not

  • Not a guarantee the next AI% or Similarity % will drop or clear school policy.
  • Not a claim that every portal PDF layout parses identically—always verify coverage and attempt ID.
  • Not “undetectable,” bypass, or advice to hide authorship.
  • Not a substitute for import-report-rewrite-flagged-only on targeting, or for match-report-span-to-rewrite on span alignment.

Where HumanPen fits

HumanPen at humanpen.net is a document-level AI revision tool: import the report (PDF or DOCX packaging), rewrite flagged continuous prose, preserve citations and layout where possible, bill by rewritten words, and offer free rounds on remaining flags under the homepage framing. Same workflow either way—not two products.
