Email badge vs portal export: open the report before you argue

Charles Li / HumanPen

An LMS email or notification card often shows a compact AI writing percentage. That number may not match what you see when you open the portal export for the same attempt. Argue from the portal report PDF with the attempt ID visible—not from the email badge alone.

This is distinct from LMS-vs-Messenger screenshots, from aligning portal badge to PDF coverage, from “screenshot ≠ case file,” from putting two tool exports side by side, and from the two-paragraph office-hours script. Here the gap is narrower: notification card vs portal export.

This note is detector-report literacy. It does not promise any score, and it is not a detection-evasion guide.

Why the card can disagree

  • Email digests can lag a re-run or a later attempt.
  • Cards often show a summary band without full continuous-prose highlights.
  • Teacher-facing mail and student portal views may refresh on different clocks.
  • Without the attempt ID, you may be comparing two different submissions.

Students in India and Southeast Asia English-medium programs often escalate from an email screenshot before they have opened the portal PDF that tutors actually use.

Do this instead

  1. Open the portal submission row; copy the attempt / submission ID.
  2. Download the full report PDF (or official export) for that ID.
  3. Compare the email % to the portal export only after IDs match.
  4. Bring the PDF—not just the email—to office hours.

What this is not

  • Not a guarantee the portal number will be lower.
  • Not advice to ignore emails your school sent.
  • Not a substitute for a formal integrity process if one is opened.

Where HumanPen fits

HumanPen at humanpen.net helps you work from a full report against your document while keeping citations and layout—useful once the portal export is the source of truth. No guaranteed next percentage.

Remember

  • Email badge ≠ final portal export.
  • Attempt ID + PDF before arguing the card.
  • Lag and summary views explain many mismatches.
  • Honest tools do not promise a clean next card.
