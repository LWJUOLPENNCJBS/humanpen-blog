Keep citations through the rewrite loop

Charles Li / HumanPen

Faced with an AI writing band on continuous prose, some students delete in-text citations, footnotes, or the whole bibliography so the file “looks more human.” That move hurts academic integrity and usually does not fix a detector band—many AI slices ignore reference lists anyway.

This is distinct from “rewrite loops are not guilt,” from making a flagged-span checklist, from keeping a long edit trail, and from how tables/appendices sit outside the AI slice. Here the rule is narrower: preserve sources while you revise prose.

This note is detector-report literacy. It does not promise any score, and it is not a detection-evasion guide.

What to keep

  1. In-text citations and footnote markers tied to claims you still make.
  2. The bibliography / reference list formatting your school requires.
  3. Quoted passages that are correctly attributed—do not strip attribution to “sound original.”
  4. Attempt ID + full export when you discuss the rewrite in office hours.

Why stripping sources backfires

Tutors in India and Southeast Asia English-medium programs read missing citations as an integrity problem first, a detector problem second. You can lose the room on honesty before the badge is even debated. Detector bands also often score continuous prose, not the reference block you just deleted.

What this is not

  • Not a guarantee the next badge will clear.
  • Not permission to invent fake citations.
  • Not a substitute for your school’s formal integrity process if one is opened.

Where HumanPen fits

HumanPen at humanpen.net supports document-level revision against a report while keeping citations and layout—so the loop does not mean “delete the sources.” No guaranteed next percentage.

Remember

  • Keep citations through the loop.
  • Stripping references ≠ fixing a band.
  • Revise flagged prose; leave the source apparatus intact.
  • Honest tools do not promise a clean next card.
