Citation tokens should not inflate the rewritten-word bill

Charles Li / HumanPen

On humanpen.net, billing and free-round effort track continuous flagged prose that was actually rewritten. Frozen citation tokens, numbers, formulas, and bibliography lines left alone should not inflate that meter. HumanPen is not a whole-document wash priced by file size.

That complements pay-by-rewritten-words (the meter is rewritten words), preserve-reference-list (back-matter stays intact), freeze-citations-numbers-formulas (in-text cites/numbers/formulas stay fixed), and only-flagged-continuous-prose (rewrite scope). It differs from free-rounds-until-under-20 / still-flagged-gets-free-rounds (loop stop), dont-wash-whole-document / leave-unflagged-prose-alone (which spans move), and docx-layout-stays-intact (layout fidelity). This note names billing honesty for frozen citations: if the tool correctly leaves (Smith, 2020) or a References line alone, those tokens are not “rewritten words.”

No promised next AI%. No “bypass detector” or “undetectable” claim. School policy still owns clearance.

Why citations must stay off the rewrite bill

  1. Citation tokens and bibliography strings are structured fidelity—not continuous prose you meant to polish.
  2. Whole-doc “humanizers” often charge by upload size or wash everything, then bill as if every token moved.
  3. If freezes work, frozen spans should not pad the paid meter or free-round effort count.
  4. Students need wallet literacy: pay for flagged prose that changed, not for (Author, Year) left untouched.
  5. Honest document-level tools align the freeze list with the billing unit.

Practical workflow (aligned with HumanPen)

  1. Import the Turnitin / iThenticate AI writing report into HumanPen.
  2. Confirm flagged continuous-prose spans; note frozen citations, numbers, formulas, and reference lines.
  3. Rewrite only flagged continuous prose; leave citation tokens and bibliography alone unless that block itself was flagged continuous prose.
  4. Expect the bill (and free-round effort, if any) to reflect words actually rewritten—not frozen tokens left intact.
  5. Re-import a fresh report for the next round; free rounds still apply only to still-flagged prose under the homepage framing.

What this is not

  • Not a guarantee the next AI% or Similarity % will drop or clear school policy.
  • Not permission to scrub or “humanize” citations to chase a lower score.
  • Not “undetectable,” bypass, or advice to hide authorship.
  • Not a substitute for pay-by-rewritten-words on the meter, or for freeze-citations-numbers-formulas on fidelity.

Where HumanPen fits

HumanPen at humanpen.net is a document-level AI revision tool: import the report, rewrite flagged continuous prose, preserve citations, reference lists, and layout where possible, bill by rewritten words, and offer free rounds on remaining flags under the homepage framing. Keeping frozen citations off the rewrite bill is part of that honesty—not an optional extra.

Remember

  • Bill / free-round effort = continuous flagged prose actually rewritten.
  • Frozen citations, numbers, formulas, and untouched bibliography lines do not inflate that meter.
  • Complements pay-by-rewritten-words, preserve-reference-list, freeze-citations-numbers-formulas, only-flagged-continuous-prose.
  • Honest tools do not sell “bypass” or “undetectable.”
