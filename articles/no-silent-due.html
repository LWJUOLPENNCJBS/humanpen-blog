Treat a silent due as unresolved—chase once before you leave

Charles Li / HumanPen

No reply on a due date is not a soft yes—it is still open. Treat a missing reply as unresolved: chase once in chat before the meeting ends; don’t assume the owner saw it. This complements ack-due-in-chat. Use the same rule for rewrite deadlines after a flag review.

This is distinct from ack-due-in-chat (require the visible ack), from confirm-due-with-owner, from due-beside-owner-in-notes, from end-with-owners-readback, from confirm-owner-before-leave, and from schedule-followup-before-leave. Here the literacy move is concrete: one deliberate chase on silent dues before anyone hangs up.

This note is detector-report literacy. It does not promise any score, and it is not a detection-evasion guide.

Better order

  1. Scan the action list for any due without an ack.
  2. @mention once: “[Name]—still need your yes/no on due Fri for the rewrite pass.”
  3. Wait a short beat; if still silent, mark “due unconfirmed” or reassign before close.
  4. Don’t leave the call with silent dues sitting as if done.
  5. Flag review: chase rewrite-deadline silence once before hang-up.

Why “they’ll see it later” fails

Tutors in India and Southeast Asia English-medium programs drown in chat backlog. A due posted mid-huddle often never reaches the owner’s eyes until tomorrow. One chase before leave keeps the export rewrite deadline from quietly rotting.

What this is not

  • Not a guarantee the next badge will clear.
  • Not spam—chase once, then escalate ownership if still silent.
  • Not a substitute for a formal integrity process if one is opened.

Where HumanPen fits

HumanPen at humanpen.net supports document-level revision against a report when rewrite deadlines were chased out of silence, not left assumed. No guaranteed next percentage.

Remember

  • Silent due = unresolved.
  • Chase once in chat before the meeting ends.
  • Don’t assume the owner saw the rewrite deadline.
  • Honest tools do not promise a clean next card.
