One pass for similarity report and AI-detector highlights

Charles Li / HumanPen

On humanpen.net, one HumanPen pass can work from a similarity report and an AI-detector highlight in the same workflow—import both, rewrite only overlapping or flagged continuous prose, and leave clean spans alone.

That complements similarity-vs-ai-two-scores (two scores, two readings), import-report-rewrite-flagged-only (report drives targeting), match-report-span-to-rewrite (rewrite the highlighted span), and recheck-after-detector-update (fresh report after vendor change). It differs from leave-unflagged-prose-alone (clean spans stay), only-flagged-continuous-prose (scope), dont-wash-whole-document (anti whole-file wash), and pay-by-rewritten-words (billing). This note names one-pass cross-detector import: similarity + AI highlights → one rewrite pass on flagged continuous prose.

No promised next AI% or Similarity %. No “bypass detector” or “undetectable” claim. School policy still owns clearance.

Why one pass across both reports

  1. Similarity and AI scores are separate meters—but students often hold both exports in one sitting.
  2. Overlapping flagged continuous prose should be rewritten once, not twice with conflicting wording.
  3. Clean spans that neither report marked should stay untouched.
  4. Whole-file wash tools ignore both maps and scrub everything.
  5. Honest tools import both maps, then bill and rewrite only the continuous prose those maps flagged.

Practical workflow (aligned with HumanPen)

  1. Export the similarity report and the AI writing report (or the combined portal export that carries both).
  2. Import both into HumanPen; confirm attempt identity and coverage match the draft you will edit.
  3. Rewrite only overlapping or flagged continuous-prose spans; leave clean neighbors, citations, numbers, formulas, and layout alone.
  4. Do not run two separate whole-document washes—one pass against the combined flagged map.
  5. Re-export; re-import a fresh report for the next round. Free rounds (if any) still apply only to still-flagged prose under the homepage framing.

What this is not

  • Not a guarantee the next AI% or Similarity % will drop or clear school policy.
  • Not permission to expand the rewrite into spans neither report flagged.
  • Not “undetectable,” bypass, or advice to hide authorship.
  • Not a substitute for similarity-vs-ai-two-scores on reading the meters, or for match-report-span-to-rewrite on span alignment.

Where HumanPen fits

HumanPen at humanpen.net is a document-level AI revision tool: import detector reports, rewrite flagged continuous prose, preserve citations and layout where possible, bill by rewritten words, and offer free rounds on remaining flags under the homepage framing. One pass across similarity and AI highlights is part of that honesty—not two conflicting whole-file rewrites.

Remember

  • Import similarity and AI highlights in one workflow; rewrite flagged continuous prose once.
  • Leave clean spans alone; do not wash the whole document.
  • Complements similarity-vs-ai-two-scores, import-report-rewrite-flagged-only, match-report-span-to-rewrite, recheck-after-detector-update.
  • Honest tools do not sell “bypass” or “undetectable.”
