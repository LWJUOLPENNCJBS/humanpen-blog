Free rounds apply only to still-flagged spans after re-import

Charles Li / HumanPen

On humanpen.net, after a paid rewrite, HumanPen free rewrite rounds apply only to spans that still show as flagged on a re-imported Turnitin / iThenticate report—not a blank-cheque to wash the whole file again. The free-round rule is scoped to leftover flags; clean sentences and non-prose stay out of scope.

That complements free-rounds-until-under-20 (the stop loop until * under 20%) and reimport-report-after-pass (why you need a fresh export). It differs from asterisk-under-20-means (what the asterisk band means), short-doc-all-or-nothing (short-file behavior), pay-by-rewritten-words (billing unit), only-flagged-continuous-prose (prose unit), dont-wash-whole-document (anti-wash framing), flagged-passages-first (first-pass priority), match-report-version-before-rerun (version match), and freeze-terminology-while-rewriting (field terms). This note names the scope of free rounds: still-flagged spans after re-import only.

No promised AI%. No “bypass detector” or “undetectable” claim. School policy still owns clearance.

What free rounds cover (and what they do not)

  1. Cover: continuous-prose spans that the new report still flags after your paid pass.
  2. Do not cover: sentences that already cleared on the new highlight map.
  3. Do not cover: tables, captions, reference lists, or other non-prose you left alone.
  4. Do not cover: a whole-document rewash “just in case”—that invents cost and risk the product does not promise for free.
  5. Stop when the re-imported report shows * under 20%—a product stop condition, not school clearance.

Practical workflow (aligned with HumanPen)

  1. Finish a paid rewrite on flagged continuous prose; freeze citations, numbers, formulas, terminology, and layout.
  2. Re-check on your school’s Turnitin / iThenticate surface; download the full new export.
  3. Re-import that fresh report into HumanPen (same attempt / version literacy as match-report-version-before-rerun).
  4. If still ≥20%, run free rewrite rounds only on remaining flagged spans—not the whole file.
  5. Repeat re-import → still-flagged free rounds until the imported report shows * under 20%. Read coverage and legend before you celebrate the asterisk.

What this is not

  • Not a guarantee the next AI% will drop or clear school policy.
  • Not unlimited free rewriting of unflagged or whole-document text.
  • Not advice to “wash until undetectable” or bypass a detector.
  • Not permission to skip re-import and guess which spans still need work.

Where HumanPen fits

HumanPen at humanpen.net is a document-level AI revision tool: import the report, rewrite flagged continuous prose, preserve citations and layout where possible, bill by rewritten words, and offer free rounds on still-flagged spans after re-import under the homepage framing. Free rounds are not a blank cheque to rewash the whole document.

Remember

  • Free rounds → remaining flagged spans on the new report only.
  • Re-import first; do not invent a second whole-file wash for free.
  • * under 20% is a product stop condition—not school clearance.
  • Honest tools do not sell “bypass” or “undetectable.”
