Similarity score vs AI score: read both before you rewrite

Charles Li / HumanPen

A high similarity percentage and a high AI writing score are not the same problem, and rewriting the wrong one wastes your free rounds.

This note is detector-report literacy for HumanPen document polish. It does not promise clearance or an undetectable rewrite. It asks you to separate similarity matches from AI-flagged prose.

Two scores, two actions

Similarity points to overlapping text with sources; AI scores highlight model-like prose. Freeze citations first, then rewrite only AI-flagged spans when that is the issue.

Badge is not a verdict—two scores need two workflows.

Steps

  1. Open the report and note similarity % and AI writing % separately.
  2. For similarity: check quotes, paraphrases, and missing citations—do not mass-rewrite.
  3. For AI flags: import the export into HumanPen and rewrite those spans only.
  4. Never treat either score as a guaranteed pass or fail.

HumanPen

HumanPen (https://humanpen.net) helps import a detection report and rewrite AI-flagged spans while keeping citations and layout. It does not sell rankings or clearance.

Remember

  • Two scores, two workflows
  • Freeze citations and numbers
  • Rewrite flagged prose only
  • HumanPen is polish help, not a pass
