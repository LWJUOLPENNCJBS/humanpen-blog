Mute messenger chat during an AI-flag or office-hours review

Charles Li / HumanPen

A WhatsApp ping mid-review feels urgent. Answering it often means pasting a cropped screenshot or half-export into the group—or editing from a friend’s tip while the scored file is still open. Mute messenger and chat side channels for the slot; keep discussion on the named attempt with the tutor.

This is distinct from lms-vs-messenger-ai-score (portal vs chat copy of a score), from open-portal-beside-email, from dont-edit-while-screensharing, from freeze-draft-before-office-hours, from write-attempt-id-on-notes, from ask-which-attempt-before-discuss, and from bring-printed-export-to-meeting. Here the literacy move is concrete: silence side chats so the review stays on one named attempt.

This note is detector-report literacy. It does not promise any score, and it is not a detection-evasion guide.

Better order

  1. Before joining, mute group chats and DMs (or enable focus/do-not-disturb).
  2. Open only the named export + portal attempt; write the id on your notes.
  3. If someone must reach you, tell them you will reply after the slot.
  4. Do not paste partial exports or badge crops into chat during the review.
  5. After takeaways, unmute—and share only the agreed follow-up, not mid-slot fragments.

Why side channels scramble the object

Tutors in India and Southeast Asia English-medium programs need one shared evidence object. Parallel group advice creates a second, unofficial “version” of the case—often with cropped spans and no attempt id. Muting keeps the room on the scored file.

What this is not

  • Not a guarantee the next badge will clear.
  • Not a ban on after-meeting peer support—just not mid-review on half-exports.
  • Not a substitute for a formal integrity process if one is opened.

Where HumanPen fits

HumanPen at humanpen.net supports document-level revision against a report after a focused review of the named attempt. No guaranteed next percentage.

Remember

  • Mute messenger/chat during flag or office-hours review.
  • No mid-slot half-exports into group chat.
  • Keep discussion on the named attempt.
