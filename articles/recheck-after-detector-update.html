Recheck after a detector update — prior free-round results go stale

Charles Li / HumanPen

On humanpen.net, when Turnitin / iThenticate (or school policy) updates the AI writing detector, treat prior free-round results as stale. Re-import a fresh report before more rewrites. Old highlights and remembered AI% no longer describe the detector your office will trust.

That complements match-report-version-before-rerun (same build before a rerun) and reimport-report-after-pass (new report after each rewrite). It differs from free-rounds-until-under-20 / still-flagged-gets-free-rounds (free-round scope), leave-unflagged-prose-alone / only-flagged-continuous-prose (what to rewrite), headings-styles-come-back / tables-captions-stay-intact (layout fidelity), and asterisk-under-20-means (product stop reading). This note names the detector-update recheck: vendor or policy change → fresh export → re-import → then decide the next round.

No promised next AI%. No “bypass detector” or “undetectable” claim. School policy still owns clearance.

Why a detector update stale-dates free rounds

  1. A model or language expansion can move highlights even when your DOCX did not change.
  2. Free-round eligibility under the homepage framing is judged against the current report—not a report from before the update.
  3. Remembered * under 20% from an old build is historical; offices will ask for the post-update export.
  4. Similarity and AI panels can diverge after vendor changes; only a fresh export shows both.
  5. Matching report version before a rerun still fails if you never re-checked after the update landed.

Practical workflow (aligned with HumanPen)

  1. Note detector name, product version, and any school notice that the AI writing model changed.
  2. Export a new Turnitin / iThenticate AI writing report on the file you hold.
  3. Re-import that fresh report into HumanPen; discard the pre-update highlight map as a plan.
  4. Rewrite only still-flagged continuous prose; freeze citations, numbers, formulas, terminology, and layout.
  5. After each pass, re-import again; free rounds (if any) apply only under the post-update report.

What this is not

  • Not a guarantee the post-update AI% will drop or clear school policy.
  • Not permission to keep rewriting from a pre-update free-round ledger.
  • Not “undetectable,” bypass, or advice to hide authorship.
  • Not a substitute for reading Similarity vs AI as two scores on the new export.

Where HumanPen fits

HumanPen at humanpen.net is a document-level AI revision tool: import the report, rewrite flagged continuous prose, preserve citations and layout where possible, bill by rewritten words, and offer free rounds on remaining flags under the homepage framing. After a detector update, honesty starts with a fresh re-import—not with arguing from a stale free-round result.

Remember

  • Detector or policy update → fresh report → re-import → then rewrite.
  • Complements match-report-version-before-rerun and reimport-report-after-pass.
  • * under 20% on an old build is history—not a post-update plan.
  • Honest tools do not sell “bypass” or “undetectable.”
