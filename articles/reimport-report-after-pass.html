Re-import the fresh AI report after each rewrite pass

Charles Li / HumanPen

On humanpen.net, a rewrite pass targets flagged continuous-prose spans from an imported Turnitin / iThenticate AI writing report. The next decision—whether a free round still applies, which spans remain hot—must use a fresh re-imported report, not your memory of the previous percentage or the previous highlight map.

That is different from import-report-rewrite-flagged-only (how the first targeting works), free-rounds-until-under-20 (the product stop loop), flagged-passages-first (priority among spans), read-highlights-before-first-edit (read before you touch prose), pay-by-rewritten-words (billing unit), short-doc-all-or-nothing, asterisk-under-20-means, keep-claim-strength-while-rewriting, freeze-citations-numbers-formulas, and similarity-vs-ai-two-scores. This note is about re-importing the new report after a pass before you plan the next round.

No promised next percentage. No “bypass detector” or “undetectable” claim. School policy still owns clearance.

Why memory of the old report fails

  1. A paid or free rewrite changes continuous prose; the old highlight map no longer describes the file you hold.
  2. The AI% badge and span list on the prior export are historical—arguing from them invents a score the detector has not re-issued.
  3. Free-round eligibility under the product framing (* under 20% stop) is judged against the current report, not the one you remember.
  4. Similarity and AI panels can diverge after edits; only a fresh export shows both.
  5. Attempt identity / coverage can change if you submitted a new file; re-import confirms you are reading the same attempt you will defend.

Practical loop (aligned with HumanPen)

  1. Import the AI writing report; confirm attempt and coverage.
  2. Rewrite highest-priority flagged continuous-prose spans (freeze citations, numbers, formulas, layout fields).
  3. Export the revised document; run or obtain a new Turnitin / iThenticate AI report on that version.
  4. Re-import that fresh report into HumanPen before deciding the next free round or which spans still need work.
  5. Repeat until the product stop condition (* under 20% on the framing) or you stop—never from remembered highlights alone.

What this is not

  • Not a guarantee the next AI% will drop or clear school policy.
  • Not permission to skip re-checking because “I already know what was flagged.”
  • Not “undetectable,” bypass, or advice to hide authorship.
  • Not a substitute for reading Similarity vs AI as two scores on the new export.

Where HumanPen fits

HumanPen at humanpen.net is a document-level AI revision tool: import the report, rewrite flagged continuous prose, preserve citations and layout where possible, bill by rewritten words, and offer free rounds on remaining flags under the homepage framing. Re-importing after each pass is how that loop stays honest—against the current report, not a remembered one.

Remember

  • After a rewrite pass → new report → re-import → then decide the next round.
  • Old % and old highlights are history, not a plan.
  • * under 20% is a product stop condition—not school clearance.
  • Honest tools do not sell “bypass” or “undetectable.”
