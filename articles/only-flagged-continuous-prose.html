Only flagged continuous prose — not tables, captions, or refs

Charles Li / HumanPen

On humanpen.net, HumanPen targets AI-flagged continuous prose spans—running sentences and paragraphs the detector highlighted—not tables, figure captions, reference lists, headings-as-labels, or sentences that already look clean. The product scope is the highlight map’s prose, not every cell or citation line in the file.

That complements dont-wash-whole-document (anti-wash framing) and differs from import-report-rewrite-flagged-only (how targeting starts), flagged-passages-first (priority order), freeze-citations-numbers-formulas (what must not move), freeze-terminology-while-rewriting (field terms), pay-by-rewritten-words (billing unit), reimport-report-after-pass / match-report-version-before-rerun (fresh-report loop), and keep-claim-strength-while-rewriting (claim force). This note names the unit of work: flagged continuous prose only.

No promised AI%. No “bypass detector” or “undetectable” claim. School policy still owns clearance.

What counts as continuous prose (for this tool)

  1. Flagged paragraph or multi-sentence spans in the body of the paper—intro, methods narrative, discussion, conclusion.
  2. Not table cells, axis labels, or numeric result grids that the report may color for coverage reasons.
  3. Not figure / table captions as a class of “must rewrite,” unless your workflow explicitly selects them.
  4. Not the reference list / bibliography lines—those stay frozen with citations and numbers.
  5. Not already-clean sentences outside the highlight map: leave them; washing them invents risk and cost.

Practical workflow (aligned with HumanPen)

  1. Import the Turnitin / iThenticate AI writing report; confirm attempt and coverage.
  2. Read highlights: mark which flags sit on continuous prose versus tables, captions, or refs.
  3. Rewrite only flagged continuous-prose spans; skip clean sentences and non-prose structures.
  4. Freeze citations, numbers, formulas, terminology, and layout while those spans change.
  5. Export, get a fresh report, re-import—still without treating the whole file as dirty prose.

What this is not

  • Not a guarantee the next AI% will drop or clear school policy.
  • Not advice to “wash until undetectable” or bypass a detector.
  • Not a claim that every highlight is prose—you still judge tables/captions/refs out of scope.
  • Not permission to hide authorship; the tool revises your flagged prose in place.

Where HumanPen fits

HumanPen at humanpen.net is a document-level AI revision tool: import the report, rewrite flagged continuous prose, preserve citations and layout where possible, bill by rewritten words, and offer free rounds on remaining flags under the homepage framing. The unit of work is only flagged continuous prose—not tables, captions, reference lists, or already-clean sentences.

Remember

  • Flagged continuous prose → rewrite; tables / captions / refs / clean sentences → leave.
  • Complements don’t-wash-whole-document: same anti-wash idea, sharper unit definition.
  • * under 20% is a product stop condition—not school clearance.
  • Honest tools do not sell “bypass” or “undetectable.”
