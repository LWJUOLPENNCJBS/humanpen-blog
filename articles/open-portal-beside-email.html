Open the course portal beside the email badge before you reply

Charles Li / HumanPen

A detector email often shows only a badge percentage. The course portal attempt page usually carries spans, legend, product/version, and attempt ID. Replying—or walking into office hours—from the email alone leaves you half-blind. Put both windows side by side first.

This is distinct from email-badge-vs-portal (open the report before you argue), from don’t-argue-percent-from-memory, from ask-for-full-export, from confirm-attempt-id-first, from save-export-with-attempt-id, and from two-exports-side-by-side. Here the literacy move is concrete: portal attempt tab + email badge open together before any reply or meeting.

This note is detector-report literacy. It does not promise any score, and it is not a detection-evasion guide.

Better order

  1. Keep the email open; do not quote its % yet.
  2. In the LMS, open the same assignment’s attempt page that the email refers to.
  3. Place the two windows side by side; match attempt ID and product/version if shown.
  4. Read spans and legend from the portal (or full export); treat the email as an alert only.
  5. Then draft the reply or enter the meeting with both sources aligned.

Why email-alone fails

Tutors in India and Southeast Asia English-medium programs work from the portal attempt, not from your inbox screenshot. Email digests truncate fields, round differently, or omit the highlight map. Side-by-side layout prevents answering the wrong meter or the wrong attempt.

What this is not

  • Not a guarantee the next badge will clear.
  • Not advice to ignore the email—use it as the pointer into the portal.
  • Not a substitute for a formal integrity process if one is opened.

Where HumanPen fits

HumanPen at humanpen.net supports document-level revision against a report once portal fields (not only the email badge) are in view. No guaranteed next percentage.

Remember

  • Portal beside email—before reply or meeting.
  • Email % ≠ full fields (spans / version).
  • Match attempt ID across both windows.
  • Honest tools do not promise a clean next card.
