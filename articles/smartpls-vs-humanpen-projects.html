SmartPLS vs HumanPen Start Project: the model you estimate versus the paper you submit

Charles Li / HumanPen · 2026-11-25

Short answer

SmartPLS 4 is an estimation engine for structural equation modelling. Its page states it is trusted in more than 30,000 published studies since 2005, lets you draw a model instead of writing syntax, and covers PLS-SEM, CB-SEM, GSCA, regression and PROCESS across more than 40 methods, each documented with its settings, results and literature.

HumanPen Start Project is not an SEM package. It takes your data or documents, proposes a plan, and returns the whole artifact: report, figures, tables, references, slides and code.

The clean division: SmartPLS answers "what are the path coefficients and are they stable". HumanPen answers "is the chapter, the table and the deck ready to hand in". If you are the methodologist building the model, you want SmartPLS in front of you. If you are the person who has to produce the defensible write-up by Friday, the model is only one of five things missing.

Why this comparison is not "which is better"

Choosing between an SEM package and a delivery service is like choosing between a calculator and a typesetter. The mistake is buying one and expecting the other.

SmartPLS's page is specific about what it does well: composite-based estimation from estimation through prediction, covariance-based SEM with bootstrapping, model comparison and multigroup analysis, GSCA estimated and bootstrapped in one workflow, and regression-based models including PROCESS and necessary condition analysis. That breadth is the reason it appears in so many published papers — the vendor cites more than 30,000 studies since 2005 and points at the standard textbooks, including the primer now in its fourth edition and the advanced-issues volume in its second.

HumanPen's page is equally specific, in a different direction: attach files, describe the need, approve a plan, then receive reports, charts and code after a second-AI check. The worked example on that page is a clinical re-analysis rather than an SEM, but the shape generalises — an endpoint comparison, a sensitivity analysis for missing outcomes, a review that passed in round one, and every reference located one by one.

The step neither tool removes

There is a step between "model estimated" and "chapter submitted" that eats days: turning output into a table a reviewer can read, rendering figures to a journal standard, writing the paragraph that interprets a coefficient without overclaiming, and keeping the numbers in the prose identical to the numbers in the table.

SmartPLS gives you the numbers and its own report view. It does not write your interpretation, and it should not — the claim strength is your responsibility.

HumanPen's stated contract touches exactly that step: figures at 300 dpi, three-line tables with captions, numbers and citations kept exactly, and a .pptx with speaker notes built from the key points and figures. For a thesis defence or a lab meeting, that last item is often the actual blocker.

Where the methods line falls

If your contribution is methodological — a new estimator, a simulation study, an invariance argument — you need a package where you can see and change every setting. SmartPLS documents each of its methods with settings, results and literature precisely for that reason.

If your contribution is substantive — a model applied to your field, a re-analysis, a report for a non-methodological audience — then the value is in the chain from data to defensible document, and the estimator being PLS-SEM or a Python implementation matters less than whether the numbers survive review.

One caveat worth stating plainly: HumanPen does not present itself as an SEM package, and its intake page frames analysis in Python or R, including re-doing work originally done in SPSS or Stata. If your design requires a specific estimator with published benchmarks in SmartPLS, ask before assuming equivalence.

Practical constraints

  • HumanPen intake supports Excel, CSV, Word, PDF and zip up to 5 GB total; Wind and CNKI data must be exported first; GPU training is not supported.
  • Projects are retained for six months after the last activity with a reminder three days before expiry, and you can delete at any time; each runs in an isolated sandbox, is not shared across users and is not used for training.
  • SmartPLS installs as a desktop application, and its trial covers all features for 30 days with only an email address required.

When to choose SmartPLS

  • You are specifying and defending the model yourself.
  • You need multigroup analysis, model comparison or invariance testing under your own control.
  • Your supervisor expects a SmartPLS project file.

When to choose HumanPen Start Project

  • The model is one of several deliverables and the write-up is the bottleneck.
  • You need the report, the figures, the deck and the code in one pass.
  • You are re-doing an analysis that lives in SPSS or Stata output and needs to become a document.

FAQ

Can HumanPen run PLS-SEM exactly as SmartPLS does? It performs analysis in Python or R. Do not assume estimator-level equivalence; describe your design and confirm the approach in the plan before work starts.

Does SmartPLS write my results section? No. It produces estimates and reports inside the application.

Which is cheaper? This article states no prices. SmartPLS's pricing path returned an empty document on 2026-11-24, and HumanPen's commercial terms are on its own pricing page.

Can I combine them? Yes: estimate in SmartPLS if your design demands it, then use HumanPen for the document layer. The plan-first gate makes the boundary explicit.
