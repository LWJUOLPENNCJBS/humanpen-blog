Write the page or paragraph number beside each flagged span in your notes

Charles Li / HumanPen

A span without a locator is hard to reopen in the export during the meeting.

This note continues detector-report literacy. It does not promise clearance. It asks for a page or paragraph locator beside each quote.

Why the locator comes first

Copy the span verbatim, then add p.12 or ¶3 from the export. Keep the attempt ID at the top of the same notes page.

Locators are logistics—not verdicts.

Four steps

  1. 1. Open the export beside your notes.
  2. 2. Quote each flagged span verbatim.
  3. 3. Add page or paragraph number beside it.
  4. 4. Never sell a guaranteed pass.
