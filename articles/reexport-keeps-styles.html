Re-export should keep heading styles and emphasis

Charles Li / HumanPen

On humanpen.net, after a flagged-only rewrite pass, re-export should keep heading styles, bold/italic runs, and paragraph spacing. HumanPen is not a plain-text dump that strips formatting and forces you to restyle the whole DOCX.

That complements docx-layout-stays-intact (layout fidelity), headings-styles-come-back (heading/style return), tables-captions-stay-intact (tables/captions), and dont-wash-whole-document (scope). It differs from freeze-citations-numbers-formulas / preserve-reference-list (citation fidelity), only-flagged-continuous-prose / match-report-span-to-rewrite (which spans move), and pay-by-rewritten-words (billing). This note names re-export style retention: polish flagged prose → styles come back with the file.

No promised next AI%. No “bypass detector” or “undetectable” claim. School policy still owns clearance.

Why re-export must keep styles

  1. Heading styles and emphasis carry structure—plain-text dumps force hours of restyling.
  2. Whole-doc “humanizers” often flatten bold/italic and collapse spacing; that is the failure mode this note rejects.
  3. Flagged-only rewrite should touch continuous prose, not reset the style sheet of the whole document.
  4. Students submit DOCX with required templates; stripped styles create new compliance work unrelated to the AI report.
  5. Honest document-level tools preserve styles on re-export by default.

Practical workflow (aligned with HumanPen)

  1. Import the Turnitin / iThenticate AI writing report into HumanPen.
  2. Rewrite only flagged continuous prose; freeze citations, numbers, formulas, tables, and layout fields.
  3. Re-export the DOCX (or PPTX where supported) and open it in your editor.
  4. Spot-check: Heading 1/2 styles, bold/italic runs outside rewritten spans, and paragraph spacing still look intentional.
  5. Re-import a fresh report for the next round; free rounds (if any) still apply only to still-flagged prose under the homepage framing.

What this is not

  • Not a guarantee the next AI% or Similarity % will drop or clear school policy.
  • Not a claim that every exotic custom style or third-party add-in will round-trip perfectly.
  • Not “undetectable,” bypass, or advice to hide authorship.
  • Not a substitute for docx-layout-stays-intact on overall layout, or for headings-styles-come-back on heading fidelity.

Where HumanPen fits

HumanPen at humanpen.net is a document-level AI revision tool: import the report, rewrite flagged continuous prose, preserve citations and layout where possible, bill by rewritten words, and offer free rounds on remaining flags under the homepage framing. Keeping styles on re-export is part of that honesty—not an optional extra.

Remember

  • After flagged-only rewrite, re-export should keep heading styles, emphasis, and spacing.
  • Not a plain-text dump that strips formatting.
  • Complements docx-layout-stays-intact, headings-styles-come-back, tables-captions-stay-intact, dont-wash-whole-document.
  • Honest tools do not sell “bypass” or “undetectable.”
