Pin the export tab before you share your screen

Charles Li / HumanPen

Sharing a busy browser window hides the export behind five other tabs.

This note continues detector-report literacy. It does not promise clearance. It asks you to pin the export tab before you share.

Why the pin comes first

Pin the tab with the attempt ID visible, then start share. When someone asks for the ID, you switch once—not hunt.

Pins are logistics—not verdicts.

Four steps

  1. 1. Open the portal export from the invite.
  2. 2. Pin that tab.
  3. 3. Start screen share with the export already frontmost.
  4. 4. Never sell a guaranteed pass.
