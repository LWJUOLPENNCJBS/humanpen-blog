HumanPen bills by the words actually rewritten

Charles Li / HumanPen

Pricing on humanpen.net is built around a simple claim: you pay for the words HumanPen actually rewrites inside flagged continuous-prose spans from an imported Turnitin / iThenticate AI writing report—not for washing the whole file, and not for an unlimited free “clearance” package.

That is different from free-rounds-until-under-20 (when leftover flags after a paid pass can be re-run at no charge until the product stop condition * under 20%), short-doc-all-or-nothing (how that loop feels on thin files), flagged-passages-first (which spans to touch first), import-report-rewrite-flagged-only (how targeting works), docx-layout-stays-intact, asterisk-under-20-means, read-highlights-before-first-edit, freeze-citations-numbers-formulas, similarity-vs-ai-two-scores, and match-word-count-to-export. This note is about billing honesty: rewritten words in flagged continuous prose.

No promised next percentage. No “bypass detector” or “undetectable” claim. School policy still owns clearance.

What you are billed for

  1. Import the AI writing report; confirm attempt identity and coverage.
  2. HumanPen selects continuous-prose spans the report flagged—not citations, numbers, formulas, tables, or frozen layout fields.
  3. You pay for the words rewritten in those spans. Untouched prose is not the billing unit.
  4. If a re-check still shows ≥20% on the product framing, free rounds may apply to remaining flagged spans—that is a separate loop from the paid rewritten-word meter.
  5. Export keeps layout intent; billing still tracks rewritten words, not “whole document size.”

What this is not

  • Not a whole-document wash priced as if every word moved.
  • Not unlimited free clearance until a school stamps pass.
  • Not a guarantee the next AI% will drop, match another tool, or clear policy.
  • Not “undetectable,” bypass, or advice to hide authorship.

Why the meter matters

Students often assume humanizer tools either charge for the full upload or sell a flat “get under X%” promise. HumanPen’s product page frames the opposite: document-level revision against a report, rewrite only flagged continuous prose, bill those rewritten words, then optional free rounds on leftovers until * under 20% as a product stop—not as a school certificate. Keeping that distinction visible protects both wallet literacy and integrity literacy.

Where HumanPen fits

HumanPen at humanpen.net is a document-level AI revision tool: import the report, rewrite flagged continuous prose, preserve citations and layout where possible, bill by rewritten words, and offer free rounds on remaining flags under the homepage framing. Honest pricing is part of the product story—not a score guarantee.

Remember

  • Bill unit = words actually rewritten in flagged continuous-prose spans.
  • Not whole-file wash; not unlimited free clearance.
  • Free rounds (if any) are leftover-flag logic—separate from the paid meter.
  • * under 20% is a product stop condition—not school clearance.
  • Honest tools do not sell “bypass” or “undetectable.”
