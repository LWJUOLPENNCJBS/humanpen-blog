Triage the flagged sentences before rewriting the whole draft

Charles Li / HumanPen

When Moodle, Canvas, or Blackboard shows an AI writing percentage, group chats often jump to “rewrite everything.” That usually wastes the next 48 hours. The percentage is a summary. The report’s highlighted passages—and the legend that explains color and confidence—are the evidence map. Start there.

This note is detector-report literacy for international students. It does not promise any score, and it is not a detection-evasion guide.

Why whole-draft panic is the wrong first move

A whole-file rewrite can change layout, break citation order, and still leave the next card ambiguous if the school’s pipeline scores only continuous prose. Students in India and Southeast Asia often burn a weekend rewriting methods tables and reference lists that never sat inside the scorable slice.

Ask a narrower question first: Which sentences does this report actually flag, at what confidence, on which attempt?

A four-step triage

  1. Open the full report, not the badge screenshot. Confirm attempt ID, file name, processing status, and legend.
  2. List high-confidence continuous-prose highlights separately from pale/uncertain bands and unscored blocks (tables, bullets, bibliography).
  3. Edit flagged prose first—clarify authorship, sources, and your own wording—then freeze citations, checked facts, and layout unless they are wrong.
  4. Re-export only after file identity is clear. Compare attempt-to-attempt with matching exports, not with a classmate’s WhatsApp crop.

Similarity is still a different instrument

A low similarity score does not cancel an AI writing indicator. An AI writing indicator does not prove plagiarism. Triage keeps those instruments apart when you talk to a tutor.

Where HumanPen fits

If you need a document-level workflow—import the report, revise flagged passages, keep citations and layout—see HumanPen at humanpen.net. It does not guarantee the next percentage, and it does not sell “guaranteed pass.”

Remember

  • Percentage = summary; highlighted sentences = evidence map.
  • Triage high-confidence continuous prose before whole-draft rewrite.
  • Freeze tables, references, and checked facts unless they are wrong.
  • No honest tool promises a clean next badge.
