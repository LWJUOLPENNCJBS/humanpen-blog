Put the scored export in full-screen during flag review

Charles Li / HumanPen

Even with one PDF open, a peek at the editor dock is tempting. Mid-span you Alt-Tab into a draft and the talk drifts. Put the scored export in full-screen or on a dedicated virtual desktop so editors and old tabs stay out of reach while you walk spans.

This is distinct from one-pdf-window-during-review (close extra export tabs), from dont-edit-while-screensharing, from freeze-draft-before-office-hours, from mute-chat-during-flag-review, from bookmark-export-url-before-reply, from write-attempt-id-on-notes, and from bring-printed-export-to-meeting. Here the literacy move is concrete: full-screen / dedicated desktop = export only while you talk.

This note is detector-report literacy. It does not promise any score, and it is not a detection-evasion guide.

Better order

  1. Open the named scored export; confirm attempt id.
  2. Enter full-screen (or move that window alone to another virtual desktop).
  3. Keep notes on a paper pad or a second device—not under the export.
  4. Exit full-screen only after takeaways; then reopen the editor if needed.
  5. If you must demo a fix, leave full-screen first and label it post-attempt scratch.

Why full-screen helps

Tutors in India and Southeast Asia English-medium programs follow what fills your screen. A half-visible Word dock invites “quick” edits mid-review. Full-screen keeps the shared object one export and one conversation.

What this is not

  • Not a guarantee the next badge will clear.
  • Not a ban on zooming or scrolling inside the export.
  • Not a substitute for a formal integrity process if one is opened.

Where HumanPen fits

HumanPen at humanpen.net supports document-level revision against a report after a focused, full-screen review of the named attempt. No guaranteed next percentage.

Remember

  • Full-screen (or dedicated desktop) the scored export during review.
  • Editors stay out of reach while you talk spans.
  • Exit full-screen before any demo scratch.
  • Honest tools do not promise a clean next card.
