The confidence / low-confidence band is not the AI writing percentage score

Charles Li / HumanPen

Many LMS cards show two visual layers at once: a headline AI writing percentage, and a confidence or low-confidence color band on highlighted spans. Students often treat the band as “another score”—or worse, as a quieter percentage they can average with the badge. That mix-up drives bad office-hours conversations.

This note is detector-report literacy. It does not promise any score, and it is not a detection-evasion guide.

Two instruments on one screen

The percentage answers a narrow question about scored continuous prose under a threshold-style estimator. The confidence band answers a different question: how sure that estimator is about particular spans. A pale band usually means uncertain, not “18% AI, soft edition.”

If the legend says low confidence / incomplete / still processing, arguing the headline number alone skips the footnote that matters most for international students in India and Southeast Asia who get WhatsApp crops without the legend.

What not to do with a pale band

  • Do not average the band with the badge into a homemade “true %.”
  • Do not treat pale spans as a free pass—or as automatic guilt.
  • Do not rewrite the whole draft because one uncertain strip looks scary in a screenshot.

A calmer reading order

  1. Open the full report; read the legend for confidence colors and incomplete markers.
  2. Separate high-confidence flagged continuous prose from pale/uncertain spans and unscored blocks.
  3. Ask your tutor which layer the module policy actually uses for decisions.
  4. If revision is required, prioritize high-confidence authored prose; freeze correct citations and layout.

Where HumanPen fits

For a document-level workflow—import the report, revise flagged spans, keep citations—see HumanPen at humanpen.net. No guaranteed next badge.

Remember

  • Confidence band ≠ AI writing percentage score.
  • Pale usually means uncertain, not a second grade.
  • Read the legend before arguing the badge.
  • Honest tools do not promise a clean next card.
