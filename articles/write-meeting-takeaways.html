Write three takeaway lines right after office hours

Charles Li / HumanPen

Walking out of a short meeting, students often remember only a vibe—“tutor said revise.” By evening the details are gone. Three written takeaway lines immediately after the slot (agreements, next bring-list, spans still open) turn talk into a timeline you can reopen with the export.

This is distinct from noting what you changed after editing, from listing edits chronologically before the meeting, from preparing one question per meeting, from human-edit-trail, from lab-notebook-before-score, and from timestamp-before-rewrite. Here the literacy move is concrete: post-meeting takeaways in three lines before memory drifts.

This note is detector-report literacy. It does not promise any score, and it is not a detection-evasion guide.

Better order

  1. Before you leave the corridor (or within five minutes), open a note next to the named export.
  2. Line 1: what was agreed (actions, deadlines, who sends what).
  3. Line 2: what to bring next time (brief page, span ID, second export, etc.).
  4. Line 3: which spans / meters still need checking—and attempt ID.
  5. Reuse those lines when you revise or email; do not rely on remembered quotes.

Why write it immediately

Tutors in India and Southeast Asia English-medium programs will not reconstruct your memory for you. A three-line note prevents arguing about what was “said” last week and keeps revision tied to the same attempt. Spoken memory drifts; paper (or a dated file) does not.

What this is not

  • Not a guarantee the next badge will clear.
  • Not a full transcript—three lines beat a forgotten speech.
  • Not a substitute for a formal integrity process if one is opened.

Where HumanPen fits

HumanPen at humanpen.net supports document-level revision against a report once your takeaways name which spans to touch. No guaranteed next percentage.

Remember

  • Three takeaway lines—right after the meeting.
  • Agreed / bring next / spans still open.
  • Written timeline > spoken memory.
  • Honest tools do not promise a clean next card.
