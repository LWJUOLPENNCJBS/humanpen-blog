Leave unflagged prose alone — only rewrite what the detector marked

Charles Li / HumanPen

On humanpen.net, spans the detector did not flag should stay as the author wrote them. HumanPen rewrites flagged continuous prose; clean sentences outside the highlight map are not a second wash target. Leaving unflagged prose alone protects author voice, billing honesty, and layout risk.

That complements only-flagged-continuous-prose (unit of work = flagged prose) and dont-wash-whole-document (anti-wash framing). It differs from flagged-passages-first (priority among flags), still-flagged-gets-free-rounds / free-rounds-until-under-20 (free-round scope), tables-captions-stay-intact (non-prose structures), freeze-citations-numbers-formulas / freeze-terminology-while-rewriting (frozen tokens), pay-by-rewritten-words (billing unit), and keep-claim-strength-while-rewriting (claim force). This note names the leave-alone rule: unflagged prose stays as written.

No promised AI%. No “bypass detector” or “undetectable” claim. School policy still owns clearance.

Why leave unflagged prose alone

  1. The highlight map already told you what the detector treated as risky continuous prose—work there first.
  2. Rewriting clean sentences invents cost (you pay by rewritten words) without a report reason to change them.
  3. Whole-file wash drifts citations, numbers, terminology, and claim strength that freeze rules were meant to protect.
  4. Author voice in clean paragraphs is often the part that already reads as human—don’t sand it flat “for luck.”
  5. After export, a fresh report may still move; washing unflagged text makes the before/after harder to explain in office hours.

Practical workflow (aligned with HumanPen)

  1. Import the Turnitin / iThenticate AI writing report; confirm attempt and coverage.
  2. Read highlights: list flagged continuous-prose spans; mark everything else as leave-alone.
  3. Rewrite only flagged continuous prose; freeze citations, numbers, formulas, terminology, and layout.
  4. Do not “improve” unflagged paragraphs while you wait for a new export.
  5. Re-import the fresh report; free rounds (if any) still apply only to leftover flags—not to the clean remainder.

What this is not

  • Not a guarantee the next AI% will drop or clear school policy.
  • Not advice to wash until “undetectable” or bypass a detector.
  • Not a claim that every unflagged sentence is perfect—only that the product scope is the highlight map.
  • Not permission to hide authorship; the tool revises your flagged prose in place.

Where HumanPen fits

HumanPen at humanpen.net is a document-level AI revision tool: import the report, rewrite flagged continuous prose, leave unflagged prose as the author wrote it, preserve citations and layout where possible, bill by rewritten words, and offer free rounds on remaining flags under the homepage framing. The leave-alone rule is the other half of “only flagged continuous prose.”

Remember

  • Unflagged prose → leave as written; flagged continuous prose → rewrite.
  • Complements only-flagged-continuous-prose and don’t-wash-whole-document.
  • * under 20% is a product stop condition—not school clearance.
  • Honest tools do not sell “bypass” or “undetectable.”
