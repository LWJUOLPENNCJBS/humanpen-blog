A crop often hides the attempt ID your tutor needs

Charles Li / HumanPen

A cropped chat image of an AI badge usually shows the percentage and little else. The fields office hours need—attempt or submission ID, export timestamp, detector name/version, and the full highlight context—are often cut off or never in frame.

This is distinct from “a screenshot is not a case file.” Here the failure mode is narrower: the crop hides identity fields that let a tutor match the badge to the LMS row.

This note is detector-report literacy. It does not promise any score, and it is not a detection-evasion guide.

What crops commonly omit

  • Attempt / submission ID on the portal card or report header.
  • Export or generation timestamp.
  • Which detector (and version) produced the badge.
  • Full continuous-prose highlights beyond the cropped pill.

Without those, tutors in India and Southeast Asia English-medium programs cannot tell whether you are debating Attempt 1 or Attempt 2, or a different file entirely.

Prefer this pack instead

  1. Full detector export PDF (or official portal download).
  2. Portal attempt / submission ID copied from the LMS row.
  3. Matching draft for that same attempt (dated copy or version history).
  4. If you must share a phone image, include the header that shows the ID—do not crop it away.

What this is not

  • Not a guarantee the next badge will clear.
  • Not advice to refuse screenshots your school already requested.
  • Not a substitute for a formal integrity process if one is opened.

Where HumanPen fits

HumanPen at humanpen.net helps you work from a full report against your document while keeping citations and layout—useful when identity fields are visible and aligned. No guaranteed next percentage.

Remember

  • Crop ≠ identity pack.
  • Attempt ID + timestamp + full PDF > cropped badge pill.
  • Match LMS row before debating the number.
  • Honest tools do not promise a clean next card.
