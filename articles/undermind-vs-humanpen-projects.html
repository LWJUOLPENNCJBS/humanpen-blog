Undermind vs HumanPen Start Project: literature discovery versus a delivered analysis

Charles Li / HumanPen · 2026-11-25

Short answer

Undermind searches the literature and reasons over it. The vendor describes it as "your AI co-researcher for the literature", claims it finds 2× as many relevant papers as AI web search, and shows a worked example ending in a research writeup with citations. That is a discovery and synthesis tool aimed at the question "what is already known".

HumanPen Start Project answers a different question: "given what is known, and given my data, what does the analysis say and what document do I hand in". It takes your files, proposes a plan, and returns a report with figures, tables, formatted references and editable slides.

If your problem is that you cannot find the right papers, Undermind is the sharper instrument. If your problem is that the papers are found but the dataset is untouched and the deadline is Friday, discovery tools leave you exactly where you are.

What a search tool can and cannot hand you

Undermind's page shows the shape of the output clearly: a query, a completed search, and a ranked set of papers — the example states that the search found 57 relevant papers — followed by a writeup in which citations are attached to claims. It also says the system fetches open-access PDFs and that you supply the rest, which is the honest boundary of any literature agent: paywalled full text is still your problem.

That is genuinely valuable work, and it is upstream of everything else. A related-work section built from a well-searched set is better than one built from whatever the first page of results returned.

But notice what is absent: nothing in that loop touches your dataset. The synthesis can tell you that an association has been reported; it cannot tell you whether the association holds in your sample, with your missingness pattern, at your sample size.

Where HumanPen sits relative to that

HumanPen's project page describes a different loop. You attach files and describe what you need. The system confirms requirements and proposes a plan; work starts only after your approval. After a check by a second AI, it delivers reports, charts and code.

The project page's worked example is a re-analysis: a six-month death-or-dependency endpoint reported at 62.2% in one arm and 63.5% in the other, with the difference not reaching significance at P = 0.072, plus a sensitivity analysis for missing outcomes, a review that passed in round one, and a note that all 11 references were found, one by one.

Two things follow. First, the deliverable is a document with numbers in it, not a reading list. Second, the reference check exists precisely because numbers and citations have to agree — which is the seam between a discovery tool and a delivery tool.

Comparing with Elicit, because buyers usually do

Elicit is the closest well-known neighbour to Undermind and is worth one paragraph here since the decision is often three-way. Elicit's page cites 138 million academic papers and 545,000 clinical trials, a semantic search that does not require the right keywords, and reports generated from a systematic-review-inspired process; it also reports a case in which a policy-facing review's data extraction matched 1,502 of 1,511 data points.

Read those three tools as one family — discovery and extraction — and HumanPen as the next stage, delivery. The practical test is simple: after the search, who runs the model, builds the table, and writes the paragraph that interprets it?

The intake detail that decides feasibility

HumanPen accepts Excel, CSV, Word, PDF and zip up to 5 GB total. Analysis runs on Python or R; work originally done in SPSS or Stata is performed in Python or R. Wind or CNKI data must be exported first, and GPU training is not supported.

That last point is a real boundary. If your project is training a model rather than analysing a dataset, neither Undermind nor HumanPen is the right instrument, and pretending otherwise wastes a week.

When to choose Undermind

  • You are entering a new field and need to know what exists.
  • You want a ranked, defensible reading set rather than a search box.
  • Your bottleneck is the related-work section or a scoping review's search step.

When to choose HumanPen Start Project

  • The dataset is in hand and unanalysed.
  • You need a report whose figures meet a journal's rendering standard.
  • You want the code delivered with the numbers so the analysis can be re-run.
  • You are re-doing an SPSS or Stata analysis and want it reproducible in Python or R.

FAQ

Can HumanPen find papers for me? No. It works on what you upload plus the task you describe. Discovery is a different product category.

Can Undermind produce my results section? It can produce a writeup grounded in the literature. It does not run your data, so it cannot produce your results.

Do they overlap at all? At the citation-checking seam, slightly: HumanPen's delivery includes a reference check. That is verification of citations inside a document, not literature search.

Is the 2× claim independently verified? No. It is the vendor's own comparison against AI web search, quoted as such.
